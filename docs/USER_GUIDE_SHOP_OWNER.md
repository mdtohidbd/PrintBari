# DocuStudio SaaS — Shop Owner User Guide

Welcome to DocuStudio SaaS — the operating system built specifically for Bangladeshi digital photo studios, photocopy shops, and cyber cafés.

---

## 1. Getting Started & Onboarding

### Registering Your Shop
1. Visit `https://app.docustudio.io/register`.
2. Fill in:
   - **Shop Name:** (e.g. *Metro Digital Color Lab*)
   - **Subdomain:** (e.g. *metro-lab* -> your shop URL will be `metro-lab.docustudio.io`)
   - **Owner Full Name & Phone Number**
   - **Email & Secure Password**
3. Click **Start 14-Day Free Trial**.
4. You receive full access to all studio tools, cloud storage, and printing queues immediately with zero upfront fees.

---

## 2. Managing Your Subscription & Billing

Navigate to **Subscription** (`/subscription`) in the sidebar:
- **Active Plan Card:** Shows your remaining trial days or next renewal date, along with storage usage and staff seats.
- **Upgrading / Renewing:**
  1. Choose **Monthly** or **Yearly** (Save 20% / ২ মাস ফ্রি).
  2. Select your desired plan:
     - **Starter Kiosk (৳990/mo):** Solo kiosk, 2 staff seats, 5 GB storage.
     - **Professional Studio (৳1,990/mo):** Multi-operator photo studio, 5 seats, 25 GB storage, vector export.
     - **Business Studio (৳3,990/mo):** High-volume agency, 15 seats, 100 GB storage, custom branding.
  3. Click **Select Plan** or **Renew Now**.
  4. In the checkout dialog, select your payment method:
     - **bKash (বিকাশ):** Instant merchant payment.
     - **Nagad (নগদ):** Postal digital payment.
     - **Cards / Internet Banking:** Visa / Mastercard via SSLCommerz.
  5. Complete payment. Your plan and quotas are upgraded immediately server-side.
- **Downloading Tax Invoices:** Go to the **Tax Invoices** tab and click **PDF** to download an official 300 DPI vector tax invoice (চালান) for shop accounting.

---

## 3. Shop Settings & Customization

Navigate to **Shop Settings** (`/settings`):
- **Shop Profile:** Upload your shop logo, set physical address, phone, and email.
- **Business Hours:** Configure opening and closing times for each day of the week.
- **Invoice Settings:** Set your custom invoice prefix (e.g. `METRO-`), starting sequence, tax ID, and footer thank-you note.
- **Print Settings:** Configure default paper size (A4 / 4x6"), printer DPI (300), and margins.

---

## 4. Staff Management & Role Delegation

Navigate to **Staff & Roles** (`/staff`):
- Click **Add Staff Member** to invite an operator.
- Enter full name, email, phone number, and assign a role:
  - **OPERATOR / STAFF:** Can process passport photos, documents, and submit print jobs.
  - **MANAGER:** Can review reports, manage customer files, and reorder print priorities.
- Deactivate staff members immediately if an operator leaves your shop.
